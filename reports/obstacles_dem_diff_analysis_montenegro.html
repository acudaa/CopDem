DEM vs. obstacle base — analysis (Montenegro)

51 obstacles compared against Copernicus DEM GLO-30 • generated by pipeline/analyze_obstacles_diff_montenegro.py from obstacles_dem_diff.csv. Field definitions: see docs/output_fields.md; obstacle-source scope and vertical-datum notes in docs/montenegro.md.

Scope, read before comparing to Austria: Montenegro's obstacle list is sourced from AIP ENR 5.4 ("Air Navigation Obstacles"), which by its own stated scope covers only permanent obstacles ≥328 FT (100 M) AGL — not a full national obstacle inventory like Austria's dataset. That's why n=51 here vs. ~1,932 for Austria: a real difference in what's measured, not a smaller country having proportionally fewer obstacles recorded. All 51 points are either antennas or wind turbines — no towers, buildings, chimneys, cranes, etc. appear in this data set, unlike Austria's broader mix. No local high-resolution ground-truth DEM (LiDAR) is available for Montenegro, so — unlike the Austria reports — this report compares against Copernicus DEM GLO-30 only.
